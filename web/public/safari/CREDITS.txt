Safari Zone assets

Layered outdoor world maps
  Ten Kanto and thirteen Hoenn outdoor towns, routes and forests use the same
  pinned FireRed and Emerald sources credited below. No additional art pack is included.
  scripts/export_world_maps.py exports paired metatile PNG atlases, Tiled JSON
  and world metadata under safari/worlds/kanto/ and safari/worlds/hoenn/.
  Each .world.json records the
  exact consumed source-file hashes and original-game-data classification.
  Original game artwork is distinct from our conversion code; a redistribution
  license for the artwork has not been established by this export.
  See docs/WORLD_MAPS.md for source formats, research and limitations.

Kanto towns, routes, forest and caves
  Pokemon FireRed assets archived by https://github.com/pret/pokefirered
  Pinned revision: ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982
  https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps
  PalletTown, Route1, ViridianCity, Route22, Route2, both Route2 forest gates,
  ViridianForest, PewterCity, Route3, Route4, MtMoon_1F/B1F/B2F, CeruleanCity.
  Native map.bin, tiles, metatiles, palettes and attributes are decoded together.
  Primary general/building tilesets and their matching secondary tilesets are
  loaded at the FireRed tile/palette offsets. General animation frame 0 is used.
  Native edge offsets and included door/ladder warps connect the journey.
  Water and unopened interiors are blocked; ledges retain directional jumping.
  Rebuild with py scripts/build_region_assets.py kanto (Pillow required).
  Recipes, generated geometry and hashes: features/safari/kanto-*.json.
  Kanto region overview is a schematic drawn in CSS/SVG for PokeSQL.

Johto towns, routes, forest, caves and gates
  Pokemon Crystal assets archived by https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal
  Pinned revision: 9ce4c4e5068ffdf56e23ca3d8dc9178b2465d482
  https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/tree/9ce4c4e5068ffdf56e23ca3d8dc9178b2465d482
  NewBarkTown, Route29, CherrygroveCity, Route30, Route31, Route31VioletGate,
  VioletCity, Route32, UnionCave1F, Route33, AzaleaTown, SlowpokeWellB1F,
  IlexForestAzaleaGate, IlexForest, Route34IlexForestGate, Route34, GoldenrodCity.
  Original 8px grayscale tiles, palette maps, daytime/environment palettes,
  per-town roof tiles/colors, 32px blocks and 16px collision quadrants are
  decoded from gfx/tilesets/, data/tilesets/ and maps/*.blk. Native map
  dimensions, connections, offsets and included warp destinations come from
  constants/map_constants.asm, data/maps/attributes.asm and maps/*.asm.
  Floor ledges retain Crystal's standing-tile jumps and diagonal direction
  masks. North-edge walls remain walkable beside their blocked edge. Cut trees
  are cleared with native floor artwork for free exploration; permanent trees,
  water and unopened interiors stay blocked. NPC/story events are omitted.
  Johto encounter pools average native daytime and nighttime grass/cave tables
  equally, using the standard 30/30/20/10/5/4/1 slot weights from Crystal.
  Source: data/wild/johto_grass.asm. No ordinary Legendary/Mythical encounters.
  Rebuild: py scripts/build_region_assets.py johto (Pillow required).
  Recipes, geometry and hashes: features/safari/johto-*.json.
  Johto region overview is a schematic drawn in CSS/SVG for PokeSQL.

Terrain maps and field effects
  Pokemon Emerald assets archived by https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald
  Pinned revision: 9103e529c2fbdd7add4a1ff3350c6c1f92112c42
  https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/tree/9103e529c2fbdd7add4a1ff3350c6c1f92112c42/data/tilesets
  Source tilesets: primary/general and secondary/petalburg, fortree, lavaridge,
  mauville, slateport and cave. General water, shore and flower animation frame 0
  is loaded at the original VRAM tile offsets from src/tileset_anims.c.
  Maps combine original 8x8 pixels using the source metatile records, flip bits,
  layers, and palettes. Route sections use original game layouts and artwork:
    forest: Route101; wetlands: Route120; ridge: Route112; meadow: Route117;
    coast: Route109; desert: Route111; cave: GraniteCave_1F;
    tundra: ShoalCave_LowTideIceRoom; volcano: JaggedPass.
  Source layout dimensions/tilesets: data/layouts/layouts.json.
  Source block data: data/layouts/<layout name>/map.bin.
  Crops and walking collisions are adapted for the PokeSQL Safari minigame.
  Native 16x16 map cells are scaled with nearest-neighbor rendering at runtime.
  grass-effect.png: graphics/field_effects/pics/tall_grass.png
  dust-effect.png: graphics/field_effects/pics/ground_impact_dust.png
  Effects retain source pixel colors; palette index 0 is made transparent.
  Rebuild with scripts/build_safari_assets.py (pillow and fonttools[woff] required).
  Recipes live in web/src/features/safari/routes.json. The build generates
  web/src/features/safari/maps.json and the matching PNGs together.

Field interface, weather overlays, and transition animation
  CSS/UI made for PokeSQL, inspired by classic Pokemon and the Wild Area.

Pokemon portraits use the bundled PokeAPI default and shiny species sprites:
https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites (sprites/pokemon and sprites/pokemon/shiny).

Registered Pokédex entries
  English game descriptions and version names are from the PokeAPI CSV dumps:
  https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/tree/master/data/v2/csv
  pokemon_species_flavor_text.csv and version_names.csv.
  Species categories use pokemon_species_names.csv through the bundled database.
  Rebuild with scripts/build_pokedex_entries.py. Highest available English version
  ID per species; game line/page breaks are normalized to spaces. Source games
  are shown with the descriptions. This catalogue is bundled for offline use.

Sinnoh: authored PokéSQL walking layouts using native Gen III terrain and
building artwork. House/lab crops come from FireRed Pallet Town; Center/Mart/
large-building and paving/bridge crops from FireRed Cerulean City. Additional
ground, trees, flowers and water use the already-vendored Emerald maps; snow and
cave pieces use the existing bundled maps. Buildings are fitted with nearest-
neighbor scaling to the unchanged authored collision footprints. These are
native Gen III art adaptations, not extracted native DS field maps.
The remaining authored field props are the fountain, construction landmark
and wind turbines, in region_architecture.py/build_sinnoh_assets.py. A native
Sinnoh model/texture pipeline is still required for those DS-specific objects.
Sinnoh's town map is original Pokemon Platinum artwork, palettes and tilemaps;
anchors are derived from its town_map_data.json, not the authored field layout.
Rebuild fields with py scripts/build_region_assets.py sinnoh.

Decorative regional trainers: native FireRed Youngster, Lass, Bug Catcher and Hiker sprites, with original object-event positions from pinned FireRed, Emerald and Crystal maps. Rebuild with py scripts/build_route_trainers.py. Four facing poses retain native pixels; these NPCs are scenery and do not initiate battles or alter collisions. Pins and per-sheet hashes: src/features/safari/route-trainers.json.

ORIGINAL GAME UI
FireRed: native text-window tiles, PC wallpapers (Forest, Beach, Cave, City),
Trainer Card background tiles and the eight Kanto badge images. Badge pictures
represent PokéSQL SQL challenge milestones; they do not award in-game Gym wins.
Platinum: native start-menu Pokedex/Pokemon/Trainer/Options icons and native
Sinnoh touch-screen town map. Sources are pinned in safari_sources.py:
https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/graphics
https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/tree/0660186c565124910371a3b0516a1b1df31b5b93/res/graphics
Rebuild with py scripts/build_game_ui.py. Per-input and per-output SHA256 hashes
are recorded in shared/ui/native-assets.json; the town-map manifest also records
its native inputs/anchors. All output PNGs ship locally and work offline.
The application/favicon/desktop icon use the bundled PokéAPI game Poké Ball PNG.
